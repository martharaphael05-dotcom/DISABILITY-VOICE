Disability Awareness

Understand. Respect. Include.

Disability experiences differ. Learning, listening and removing barriers help everyone participate more fully.

♿

Physical Disabilities

Physical disabilities may affect mobility, coordination or physical activities. Accessible buildings, transport and digital services support participation.

◉

Visual Impairments

People may have low vision or blindness. Useful access includes screen-reader support, good contrast, alternative text and accessible documents.

◖

Hearing Impairments

Communication access can include captions, sign-language interpretation, clear written information and suitable listening environments.

◎

Intellectual and Developmental Disabilities

Clear communication, inclusive learning, patience and appropriate support can help people participate and make choices.

↝

Spinal Cord Injury

A spinal cord injury can affect movement and sensation in different ways. Experiences and support needs vary from person to person.

＋

Non-visible and Other Disabilities

Some disabilities are not immediately apparent. Respect privacy, avoid assumptions and ask what access support is useful.

Principles of Inclusion

  • Rights: dignity, choice and equal treatment.
  • Accessibility: remove physical, communication and digital barriers.
  • Participation: include persons with disabilities in decisions.
  • Respect: listen without pity, stereotypes or assumptions.

A Lived-experience Perspective

From a Change of Direction to a New Way Forward

Before my accident, my work and ambitions were closely connected to geology and borehole drilling. A road accident in 2019 and a cervical spinal cord injury changed the way I move through the world and the kind of work I can do in the field.

My story is not about asking for pity. It is about adapting, learning, faith, access, and continuing to contribute. I want people with disabilities to be heard, recognised and included—and I want communities to focus on the barriers that can be changed.

Advocacy and Action

Move beyond awareness. Build access.

Advocacy should lead to practical changes in the places where people learn, work, travel, receive services and connect online.

01

Accessible Environments

Promote ramps, lifts, accessible toilets, safe paths, inclusive transport and thoughtful event planning.

02

Education and Digital Access

Encourage accessible learning materials, digital skills, assistive technology and inclusive online experiences.

03

Employment and Independence

Support fair recruitment, reasonable adjustments, remote-work access and meaningful economic participation.

04

Healthcare and Rehabilitation

Raise awareness of respectful care, rehabilitation, assistive devices and individual support needs.

05

Representation and Policy

Include persons with disabilities in conversations, research, planning and decisions that affect their lives.

06

Community and Peer Support

Create spaces where people can exchange knowledge, encourage one another and find reliable resources.

A Personal Journey

Embracing Life's Detour

My interests span geology and borehole drilling, digital work, social media management, AI-assisted image and video creation, and disability advocacy. These parts of my journey remind me that a change in circumstances can call for new tools, new routes and new ways to contribute

The talk concept “In August 2026, I delivered a TEDx talk titled “Embracing Your Life’s Detour: From Boreholes to Bandwidth,” where I shared how my spinal cord injury changed my career path and led me to explore digital opportunities and technology. My message encourages people to embrace life's unexpected changes, recognise their value, and continue pursuing meaningful goals. Through my story and disability advocacy, I hope to promote accessibility, digital inclusion, and a society where persons with disabilities can participate fully and contribute meaningfully.

The purpose is not to present disability as the end of a person's story. It is to encourage people to recognise their value, adapt where necessary and work towards a society that makes participation possible.

Founder / Advocate Profile

Raphael Martha

I am a Geology graduate of Prince Abubakar Audu University (formerly Kogi State University), with experience in geology and borehole drilling. Following my spinal cord injury, which left me using a wheelchair, I could no longer work at drilling sites. Rather than allow my condition to limit me, I enrolled in courses and developed new skills to work remotely as a virtual assistant and social media manager, while building expertise in AI-generated images and videos. As a wheelchair user and spinal cord injury survivor, I am passionate about disability rights, accessibility, rehabilitation, education, employment, and digital inclusion, particularly in Nigeria. Through Disability Voices, I aim to amplify lived experiences, share valuable information and opportunities, and promote practical actions toward a more accessible and inclusive society.
