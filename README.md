# Disability Voices

### Amplifying Voices. Promoting Inclusion. Creating Opportunities.

## About Me

I am a Geology graduate of Prince Abubakar Audu University (formerly Kogi State University), with a background in geology and borehole drilling.

Following my spinal cord injury, which left me using a wheelchair, I could no longer work at drilling sites. Rather than allow my condition to limit me, I enrolled in courses and developed new skills to work remotely as a virtual assistant and social media manager. I also developed expertise in AI-assisted image and video creation.

## My Interests

- Geology and borehole drilling
- Virtual assistance and remote work
- Social media management
- AI-generated images and videos
- Disability rights and advocacy
- Accessibility and digital inclusion
- Education, rehabilitation, and employment opportunities for persons with disabilities

## My TEDx Journey – 2026

In August 2026, I delivered a TEDx talk titled *"Embracing Your Life's Detour: From Boreholes to Bandwidth."*

Through this talk, I shared how my spinal cord injury changed my career path and led me to explore technology, digital skills, and new professional opportunities.

My story highlights the importance of adapting to unexpected life changes, recognising our value, and finding new ways to contribute to society.

## The Mission of Disability Voices

Through Disability Voices, I aim to amplify the lived experiences of persons with disabilities, share useful information and opportunities, challenge limiting perceptions, and promote practical actions toward a more accessible and inclusive society.

I believe disability should not prevent anyone from learning, working, expressing themselves, or contributing meaningfully to society.
